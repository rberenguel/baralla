### Goal

Capture all Enemy Combatants (Aces and Face Cards) using the enemy's own weapons and tools (non-face cards) against them.

### Decks

- **Personal Draw Deck** — all 2s, 3s, 4s + 2 Jokers (14 cards). Your resources.
- **Enemy Draw Deck** — all 5s–10s, Jacks, Queens, Kings, Aces (40 cards). The opposition.

### Round (4 Phases)

1. **Enemy Phase** — Slide existing enemy cards right to fill gaps; draw from the Enemy Deck to refill empty positions left-to-right (Position 4 → 1). Skip if the Enemy Deck is empty.
2. **Discard Phase** — Discard any number of cards from your hand to your Personal Discard pile.
3. **Draw Phase** — Draw from your Personal Deck until you hold 4 cards. If your deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile into a new Personal Deck first.
4. **Capture Phase** — Do exactly one of:
   - **Capture**: Spend cards from your hand totalling ≥ the enemy card's value, same suit. All spent + captured cards go to your Personal Discard.
   - **Let Be Captured**: Remove the Position 1 enemy card + one card from your hand; place both in the Enemy Capture Pile (out of game). Beware: if any Ace or Face Card reaches the Enemy Capture Pile, you lose immediately.
   - **Sacrifice**: Remove any 2 cards from your hand to the Enemy Capture Pile; send one enemy card from the row to the bottom of the Enemy Deck. You may NOT sacrifice Aces or Face Cards.

### Card Values

| Card  | Value |
| ----- | ----- |
| 2–10  | Face  |
| Jack  | 11    |
| Queen | 12    |
| King  | 13    |
| Ace   | 14    |
| Joker | Copies any card already in your hand |

### Win / Lose

- **Win**: Enemy Deck exhausted, all 4 Enemy Positions empty, no Ace or Face Card in the Enemy Capture Pile.
- **Lose**: Any Ace, Jack, Queen, or King reaches the Enemy Capture Pile.
